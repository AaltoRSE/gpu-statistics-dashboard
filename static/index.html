Metric glossary

Mean utilization
Time-weighted mean GPU utilization over the window. GPU devices are collapsed to a per-job/per-node max first, so this measures “how busy” a job, node or GPU type was — not raw GPU-hours.
Mean occupancy
A GPU type’s window-average share of its total GPU capacity (every node carrying that type, idle included) that had an active job on it, regardless of how busy that job's GPUs actually were. A type can have high occupancy and low utilization (jobs running but idle) or the reverse.
GPU-hours (Users table)
Utilization-weighted GPU-hours: mean utilization × GPU time. Ranks users by how much they actually used, not how much they were allocated. Not the same figure as a job's effective GPU-hours below.
Effective GPU-hours (a user's jobs)
Effective GPU-hours: allocated GPU-hours (gpus × elapsed time) × mean utilization, once sacct/scontrol metadata resolves the allocation. Before that resolves, a Prometheus-only estimate is shown instead. This is allocation-based, unlike the Users table's GPU-hours above.
Allocated vs. effective GPU-hours (VRAM chart)
Allocated GPU-hours is gpus × elapsed time from sacct — what was reserved, regardless of use. Effective GPU-hours is that figure × mean utilization — what was actually used. The VRAM chart stacks them so the allocated-but-idle gap is visible directly; a job with no sacct allocation record is excluded from the chart and counted separately in its caption.
